About Me

As a geographer specializing in community ecology, I bridge the gap between spatial dynamics and functional biodiversity. My research employs a trait-based approach to analyse macroecological patterns and understand the specific footprint of human activities on the Vertebrates.

I frame my work within the context of the Anthropocene, addressing conservation challenges such as human-wildlife coexistence or functional erosion. I am particularly interested in how selective exploitation targets some traits combinations, alters ecosystem functioning and biodiversity at multiple scales.

Beyond the field and the data, I am deeply committed to an inclusive, collaborative, and international scientific practice. When I'm not on the field, teaching or coding in R, you'll find me hiking with my dog and camping in the mountains.
